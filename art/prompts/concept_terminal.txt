A complete 16:9 game-screen mockup for a grown-up idle clicker game about a programmer who lets an AI coding assistant do all his work while he only presses "Yes". The whole game looks like the refined terminal window of an AI coding assistant.

STYLE: flat, precise interface design. Dark warm charcoal background (#1F1E1D). Panels slightly lighter (#262624) with thin 1px rounded borders (#3D3A36). Crisp monospace typography in warm off-white (#F0EEE6) and muted grey (#87867F). One coral accent (#D97757) reserved for the AI and its prompts. Calm green (#6FB07F) reserved for money. Thin lines only, generous spacing, quiet and serious, like a premium developer tool. NO glow, NO neon, NO cartoon characters, no gradients, no 3D.

LAYOUT:
- Top bar: at the left three small dots and a short title bar; in the centre a large monospace amount "$1,284" in green with a smaller "+12.5/s" next to it.
- Main area (left 70%): a terminal log made of rows of thin placeholder bars of different lengths, a few rows starting with a small coral square marker. In the middle of it a bordered permission box: one line of placeholder bars, then three option rows; the first option is highlighted with a coral marker and reads "Yes". Below the box: a row of five small squares (two filled coral) and a large rounded keycap labelled "Enter".
- Lower left of the main area: a small diagram made of thin lines: one laptop outline connected by thin lines to four small boxes, with tiny coral squares travelling along the lines.
- Right panel (30%): a vertical list of five rows like a package list; each row has a tiny line glyph, a name shown as placeholder bars, a count, and a price in green digits; the last two rows are dimmed.
- Bottom: a thin one-line status bar with placeholder bars.

All text except "$1,284", "+12.5/s", "Yes", "Enter" and the small prices is shown as thin placeholder bars, never as letters.
