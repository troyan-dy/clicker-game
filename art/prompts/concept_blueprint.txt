A complete 16:9 game-screen mockup for a grown-up idle clicker game about a programmer who lets an AI assistant do all his work while he only presses "Yes". The whole game looks like a deadpan-serious technical infographic of his growing operation.

STYLE: clean isometric line diagram, like an architecture diagram in an engineering report. Warm ivory paper background (#F0EEE6) with a very faint dot grid. Thin precise charcoal lines (#262624) of one fine weight. Flat muted fills used sparingly: terracotta (#D97757) as the single strong accent, plus soft sage (#BCD1CA), dusty blue (#6A9BCC) and sand (#D4A27F). Calm, exact, a little ironic. NO thick outlines, NO chunky cartoon shapes, NO candy colours, no gradients, no realistic shading.

SCENE (centre and left, about 70% of the width): in the middle, an isometric desk with an open laptop and a small simply drawn seated programmer leaning back with a mug. On the laptop screen: a small terracotta square with two dot eyes (the AI). Thin connector lines run from the laptop to several small isometric objects placed around it: two more laptops, a small office building, a server rack; each has a tiny numbered tag. Small terracotta squares travel along the connector lines toward the laptop and then up toward the top of the screen. In front of the desk, a single terracotta keyboard key drawn isometrically.

INTERFACE in the same quiet style:
- Top centre: the amount of money, large, in an elegant serif typeface: "$1,284" in deep green ink (#3F7D58), with a smaller line under it.
- Near the laptop: a small callout box with a thin leader line, three thin grey placeholder bars, five small square checkboxes (two filled terracotta) and a neat keycap labelled "Yes".
- Right side: a narrow legend-like panel separated by one thin line, with three tidy cards; each has a tiny isometric line icon, two thin grey placeholder bars and a small price in serif digits.
- Bottom: one thin line and a row of small grey placeholder bars (a news ticker).

All text except "$1,284", "Yes", the tiny number tags and the three small prices is shown as thin grey placeholder bars, never as letters.
