A small banner illustration in the style of a tech-startup landing page ("corporate Memphis" flat illustration), used as a parody of company websites.

- Completely flat shapes with NO outlines at all.
- Two or three faceless people with tiny heads and huge, long, bendy arms and legs, happily high-fiving around a floating laptop.
- Simple geometric decorations around them: circles, squiggles, a potted plant, a rocket.
- Pastel background (soft cream or pale lilac) with exactly one acid accent colour (bright mint or hot pink).
- No text, no letters, no logos, no buttons. Edge to edge, no frame.
- Friendly, generic, slightly too enthusiastic.

This asset deliberately does NOT follow the outlined cartoon style of the rest of the game: flat, outline-free, faceless.
